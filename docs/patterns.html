Combined patterns

Each row is a rule found by a shallow decision tree over every indicator. "+7% rate" = share of stocks matching the rule that rose 7% or more the following week. Years confirmed counts the separate hidden years in which the rule beat that year's normal rate. One-year rules are usually a single event (like a crash rebound), not a repeatable pattern.

Every indicator, in ten buckets
